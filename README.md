
Project Title

U.S. Coca-Cola Retailer Performance & Seasonal Sales Optimization (2021)

Executive Summary

This project analyzes 2021 beverage sales, unit volume, pricing, and operating profitability across 4 major U.S. retail partners (Sodapop, FizzySip, BevCo, and DreamCo) spanning 51 cities and all 50 states. The primary goal is to uncover seasonal demand drivers, regional performance gaps, and brand-level profitability to optimize inventory allocation and retail strategy.
